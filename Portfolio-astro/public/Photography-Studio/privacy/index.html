What we collect

When you send the booking form we receive your name, email address, preferred date, chosen package, and your message. We use them only to reply to you and plan your session. If you pay a deposit or order a print, payment is taken by Stripe; we never see your card number, only your name, email, shipping address (for prints), and the amount paid. Confirmation emails are sent through Resend.

We also count page views. The count records the page address and, if you arrived from another site, that site's name, once per visit. It stores nothing in your browser, does not record your IP address, and is skipped if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

What we do not do

  • This site sets no tracking cookies.
  • It loads no advertising or third-party scripts. Stripe's payment page is a separate site you are sent to when you pay.
  • We do not sell or share your details.

Where the form goes

The form is handled by the site's host. Messages are kept for as long as needed to run your session, then deleted on request.

Your choices

Ask us to see, correct, or delete what we hold about you by emailing the address on the contact form. The "Calm mode" setting is stored only in your own browser.
