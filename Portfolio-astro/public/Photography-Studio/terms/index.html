Booking

A session is booked once the deposit is paid or we confirm the date and package in writing. You can move a booked date once, at no cost, using the link in your confirmation email, as long as the new date follows our usual notice period.

Weather and light

Outdoor sessions depend on the weather. If conditions are unsafe or the light is not workable, we will offer a new date.

Payment

A deposit holds your date and counts toward the price of your session. The balance is due on the day of the session and can be paid online from the link in your emails.

Cancellations and refunds

Cancel at least 14 days before your session and the deposit is refunded in full to the card you paid with; refunds usually reach you in 5 to 10 days. Cancelling less than 14 days before keeps the deposit. If we cancel, or weather makes the session unworkable and we cannot agree a new date, you receive a full refund. The number of days is set in the studio's booking rules, so keep this page in step with them.
