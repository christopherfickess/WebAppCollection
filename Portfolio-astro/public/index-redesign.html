I build cloud infrastructure that's meant to disappear: distributed systems and high-availability Kubernetes platforms that stay boring to operate, even under load. 6+ years doing this hands-on, the last 3+ as a technical lead, which means as much time setting standards and mentoring as writing Terraform.

Infrastructure as code and GitOps are where I'm strongest: environments defined in git, deployed the same way every time, with drift treated as a bug to fix rather than a fact of life. I'm most effective on cost efficiency, deployment automation, and the operational discipline (observability, incident response, disaster recovery that's actually been tested) that makes a platform trustworthy instead of just functional.

Security gets designed in, not bolted on afterward: zero-trust networking, least-privilege IAM, encryption by default, and compliance frameworks like DoD IL5 and FedRAMP treated as design constraints from day one rather than a checklist at the end.

Design notes: what changed from the current page, and why
  • Kept the illustration, reframed it. It was floating loose against the gradient blob before. Now it sits in its own card (same border/shadow language as the status panel), slightly desaturated, and several of its fills were switched from hardcoded hex to your actual theme variables (var(--accent), var(--bg-soft), var(--border)), so it now recolors with the light/dark toggle instead of staying static.
  • Added a real status panel next to it (uptime / environments / deploys / IaC coverage) built from plain CSS. It's currently illustrative only and not wired to anything live yet. See the note on the card itself and the open question about syncing it to the Starlight cluster safely.
  • Dropped the neon cyan→magenta gradient heading. It read as a generic "dev portfolio template" cue. Replaced with a small accent-colored kicker label ("SITE RELIABILITY ENGINEER") above a solid-color serif name: a more restrained, editorial hierarchy that still uses your existing brand font and accent blue.
  • Skill list to monospace tags (#aws #eks #terraform #gitops #zero-trust) instead of three stacked lines, and pulled from your actual resume's weighting rather than generic labels.
  • Bio rewritten from your resume (~/git/personal/latex-resume-cf): years of experience, the Boeing EKS/Karpenter/IaC numbers, and the security/observability stack, instead of generic "my goal is to..." filler.
  • Added real call-to-action buttons ("Get in Touch", "View GitHub") instead of only two small icon links.
  • Swapped the big gradient-blob background for a subtle dot-grid. Theme-aware instead of hardcoded.
  • Footer is now pinned to the bottom of the viewport on short pages (flex layout + margin-top: auto) instead of floating mid-page, and shrunk further (icons now 1.5rem).
  • Rail nav, fonts, and light/dark system are untouched. This is a hero-section concept, not a navigation change.
